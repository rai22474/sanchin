# Artifacts

[README](../../README.md) · [Docs](../index.md)

The documents the process produces, what each must contain, and which rules
the validator enforces on them. Names, keys, headings and states are the
defaults; a project renames them in its adapter ([adapter.md](../adapter.md)).
Starting points live in [templates/](../../templates/index.md). Read only the
format you are about to write or review.

| Document | What it contains | When to read it |
|---|---|---|
| [base-documents.md](base-documents.md) | Mission and vision, stack, constitution, glossary: what each says and who reads it. | Setting up a project; when a role can't find a principle. |
| [story.md](story.md) | The story's frontmatter, sections and rules by state. | Product owner writing or refining a story; anyone reviewing one. |
| [scenarios.md](scenarios.md) | Feature files, trace comments, the `@pending` tag. | Product owner writing scenarios; developer making them red. |
| [plan.md](plan.md) | The plan's sections, constitution checklist and rules by state. | Tech-lead writing a plan; developer and reviewer reading one. |
| [layers-and-roadmap.md](layers-and-roadmap.md) | Epics and narratives, and the roadmap's milestones with their generated tables. | Product owner placing stories; anyone reading the roadmap. |
| [issue.md](issue.md) | The issue as the spec of a bug fix. | Maintainer. |
