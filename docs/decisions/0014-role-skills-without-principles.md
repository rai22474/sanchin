# 0014 — Role skills that hold no principles of their own

[README](../../README.md) · [Decisions](index.md)

**Status:** accepted (2026-10-31)

## Decision
Seven skills named by team role: `init`, `product-owner`, `architect`,
`developer`, `maintainer`, `reviewer`, `coach`. They say how to reason and
what to produce; the project's principles come from its base documents
(mission and vision, stack, constitution, glossary). Advisory skills end by
offering to write their conclusion as a draft (proposal, decision record).

## Why
Principles copied into a skill go stale when the project changes them, and
the skill then argues against the project. Role names tell a newcomer what
each skill does without reading it.
