# 0016 — Every rule carries a general why, never an anecdote

[README](../../README.md) · [Decisions](index.md)

**Status:** accepted (2026-10-31)

## Decision
Each rule states the general reason it exists. The incident that produced
it stays in the project's issues, not in the process.

## Why
An agent matches an anecdote by its surface shape and misses the same
problem in a different shape. A general reason applies to cases nobody has
seen yet — and keeps project details out of a public plugin.
